What PF Pension Assistant Provides

PF & Pension Knowledge Hub

Comprehensive step-by-step guidance on Member Portal navigation, passbook viewing, online transfer Form 13, and withdrawal procedures (Form 19, 10C, 31).

EPS 95 Pension Guidance

Understand pension calculation methodologies, eligibility criteria (10+ years of service), scheme certificate options, and life certificate (Digital Jeevan Pramaan) requirements.

Grievance Advisory

Understand how to draft and file official grievance petitions through EPFiGMS (EPFO Grievance Management System) and track pending claim issues.
