Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 18, 2026 • Published by AlgoAuraX for PF Pension Assistant (Android)

Target Audience: 18+ (Working professionals & pensioners) Contact: sachinvr141@gmail.com
Privacy Commitment: PF Pension Assistant is built with a privacy-by-design architecture. We never ask for, store, or transmit your Aadhaar card number, UAN password, OTPs, or EPF portal login credentials.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which PF Pension Assistant (the "Application"), developed and maintained under the brand AlgoAuraX ("we", "us", or "our"), collects, uses, maintains, and discloses information collected from users ("User" or "you") of the Android application and this legal portal website.

By downloading, installing, accessing, or using PF Pension Assistant, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the Application.

2. Information We Collect (Verified Application Behavior)

We adhere strictly to data minimization principles. Our code architecture deliberately restricts data collection to what is strictly required to provide functionality and support:

A. Optional Account Information (Firebase Authentication)

If you choose to sign in to the Application using Google Sign-In, we receive basic authentication token details provided by Google Credential Manager and Firebase Authentication:

  • Your Google account display name
  • Your verified Google email address
  • Your Google profile picture URL (if provided by your Google account profile)

These details are used solely to personalize your local interface and authenticate your session. We do not access your Google contacts, drive, emails, or personal calendar.

B. PF Expert Assistance Service Intake Information

When you choose to request the paid "PF Expert Assistance" service (₹299), you voluntarily submit the following details into the application's intake form:

  • Full Name: To identify the member or inquirer
  • Mobile Number: 10-digit Indian mobile number for communication
  • Assistance Category: The topic of inquiry (e.g., PF Claim Settlement, EPS 95 Pension, Joint Declaration, Transfer / KYC)
  • Problem Description: A written summary of your issue or question
  • Transaction References: Generated unique Request ID (e.g., REQ-xxxxxx), payment order reference, and payment ID returned upon successful checkout
CRITICAL NOTICE — STRICTLY PROHIBITED SENSITIVE DATA:
We DO NOT collect, store, or request:
  • Universal Account Number (UAN) passwords or Member Portal credentials
  • Aadhaar card numbers or biometric records
  • EPFO SMS One-Time Passwords (OTPs)
  • Bank account login credentials, debit/credit card PINs, or UPI PINs
Users are explicitly instructed NEVER to input, submit, or message passwords or OTPs to our support team or anyone else.

3. Data Storage & Local Persistence Architecture

Transparency regarding how and where your data lives:

  • On-Device SQLite Database: The intake requests and transaction identifiers for PF Expert Assistance are stored locally on your Android device in a secure SQLite database (ExpertRequestDbHelper). This allows your service history and Request ID to persist across app updates, device restarts, and offline periods.
  • No Cloud Synchronized User Database: The Application does not currently maintain a centralized remote database synchronizing your entire personal device profile to third-party cloud servers.
  • WhatsApp Communications: When you tap the button to proceed to WhatsApp support, the verified details (Request ID, Name, Mobile, Service Category, Problem Summary, and Payment Reference) are formatted into an encrypted WhatsApp text message. Note: The message is only transmitted when you manually press "Send" inside the WhatsApp application.

4. Third-Party Service Providers & Payment Gateways

The Application interacts with reputable third-party infrastructure providers to perform specific operational functions:

  • Google Play Services & Firebase: Provides Google Sign-In session management and core runtime compatibility. Firebase operates under Google's stringent privacy standards.
  • Razorpay (Payment Gateway): When purchasing the ₹299 service, payments are processed by Razorpay Software Private Limited. We do not collect or store full credit/debit card numbers or net banking credentials; these are handled directly on Razorpay's PCI-DSS compliant secure infrastructure.
  • WhatsApp Business (Meta): Customer support interactions take place over WhatsApp. Messages sent over WhatsApp are subject to WhatsApp's Terms of Service and End-to-End Encryption protocol.

5. How We Use Collected Information

We process your information exclusively for the following purposes:

  1. To operate and deliver the features of the PF Pension Assistant app.
  2. To provide the ₹299 personalized procedural advisory service requested by you.
  3. To verify payment status, reconcile transactions, and prevent fraudulent claims.
  4. To communicate with you via WhatsApp or Email regarding your specific support ticket.
  5. To comply with statutory accounting and tax obligations applicable under Indian law.

6. Data Retention Policy

Local Data: Form submissions and request records saved in your device's local SQLite database remain on your phone until you clear the application's storage via Android Settings or uninstall the app.

Support Records & Accounting Data: Communications sent to our support email or WhatsApp number, along with Razorpay financial settlement records, are retained for a minimum period of 3 years to comply with financial audits, consumer dispute resolution, and applicable taxation regulations, after which they are securely purged.

7. User Rights & Data Deletion

Every user retains full rights regarding their personal data under applicable data privacy frameworks:

  • Right to Erase / Delete: You can request immediate erasure of your support conversations, email records, and stored customer references by contacting us. Detailed steps are provided on our dedicated Account and Data Deletion Page.
  • Right to Rectify: If you supplied an incorrect mobile number or email in your assistance request, you can contact our support team to update your record.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may discontinue using the service or disconnect your Google account at any time.

8. Children's Privacy

PF Pension Assistant is designed for working employees, PF subscribers, and pensioners eligible for retirement benefits. It is not intended for or directed toward children under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

9. Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security practices, including TLS/HTTPS network encryption, secure Android Credential Manager tokenization, and strict prohibition of plaintext password/OTP storage. However, please remember that no method of electronic storage or mobile transmission is 100% immune from security threats.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our operational practices, new app releases, or evolving regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted to this URL with a revised "Effective Date" at the top of the document.
