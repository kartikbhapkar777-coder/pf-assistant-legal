Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 26, 2026 • Published by AlgoAuraX for PF Pension Assistant (Android)

Target Audience: 18+ (Working professionals & pensioners) Contact Email: sachinvr141@gmail.com
Privacy Commitment: PF Pension Assistant is built with strict privacy-by-design standards. We NEVER ask for, collect, or store your EPFO UAN password, Aadhaar OTP, or banking passwords.

1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how PF Pension Assistant (the "Application"), developed and maintained under the brand AlgoAuraX ("we", "us", or "our"), handles personal information. This policy applies to the Android application (Package: com.algoaurax.pfpensionassistant) and this official support website.

2. Zero Sensitive Credential Storage

CRITICAL PRIVACY SAFEGUARD:
We NEVER ask for, collect, or store your EPFO UAN password, Aadhaar OTP, or banking passwords.

Users are strictly instructed never to share passwords, OTPs, or financial secrets with our app, support staff, or any third party. The Application does not connect directly to EPFO servers to capture credentials.

3. Information Collected & Authentication Flow

We adhere strictly to Google Play Data Safety requirements and minimal data collection:

A. Google Sign-In via Firebase Authentication

When a user signs in, Google Sign-In via Firebase Auth collects only basic profile information:

  • Email Address: Used strictly for secure account identification.
  • Display Name: Used to personalize the app interface.

This data is encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-standard Firebase infrastructure. We do not access your Google contacts, Google Drive, or external account services.

B. Assisted Service Queries (₹299 Advisory)

When users request our optional ₹299 assisted advisory service, contact details are submitted securely:

  • Name and Phone Number: Submitted through the assisted service form for support communication.
  • Query Message & Category: Description of the procedural issue (e.g., claim rejection reason, joint declaration inquiry).
  • Sole Purpose: Contact details are used solely for query resolution and support follow-up.
  • No Advertising Sharing: Data is never sold, rented, or shared with third-party advertisers or marketing data brokers.

C. Calculations & Estimations

All EPS-95 pension calculations and EPF compound interest projections run locally on-device. Input parameters (wages, years of service) are processed in volatile memory and are not uploaded to our servers.

4. Security Standards

We implement industry-standard security measures, including HTTPS/TLS encryption for all network transmissions and Firebase Cloud security protocols. Local database records (if any) are restricted by Android's sandbox permissions.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

  • Google / Firebase: Authentication infrastructure and runtime hosting.
  • Razorpay Software Private Limited: Secure payment gateway processing for the ₹299 consultation fee. Payments occur on PCI-DSS certified infrastructure; we never store full card numbers or banking passwords.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): Optional user-initiated support communication protected by WhatsApp's end-to-end encryption.

6. User Rights & Data Deletion Process

Every user has the right to access, rectify, or request permanent deletion of their inquiry records. To delete your contact details or support ticket history:

  • Visit our dedicated Account and Data Deletion Page.
  • Email your request directly to sachinvr141@gmail.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request - PF Pension Assistant.
  • Message WhatsApp support at +91 9921200935.

Verified requests are processed and permanently purged from support records within 48 business hours.
