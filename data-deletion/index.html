Account and Data Deletion

Instructions for requesting deletion of personal data and associated records under AlgoAuraX.

Dedicated Support: sachinvr141@gmail.com WhatsApp: +91 9921200935

1. Our Data Retention & Deletion Principles

At AlgoAuraX, we respect your right to control your personal information. In full compliance with Google Play's User Data policy on Account Deletion, this page provides a clear, publicly accessible, and straightforward path for users of PF Pension Assistant to request the permanent deletion of their account records and associated personal data.

2. What Data Is Stored & Where

  • On Your Device (Local Storage): The intake requests and transaction records are stored in your device's local SQLite database. You can instantly delete all local data at any time by clearing the app data through your phone settings: Settings > Apps > PF Pension Assistant > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Support Communications: Emails sent to our support inbox and messages exchanged on our official WhatsApp desk.
  • Payment Transaction Logs: Razorpay payment identifiers and order IDs associated with the ₹299 service.

3. Step-by-Step Manual Deletion Request Process

Because the Application operates with on-device persistence rather than an automated cloud user-management backend, data deletion requests are reviewed and processed manually by our privacy team to ensure all associated records are thoroughly purged.

Option A: Request Deletion via Email (Recommended)

Send an email from your registered email address to our data privacy desk:

Open Pre-filled Deletion Email

Or write to: sachinvr141@gmail.com with the subject Account and Data Deletion Request - PF Pension Assistant.

Option B: Request Deletion via WhatsApp Support

You may also contact our verified WhatsApp support desk to submit your deletion request:

Message WhatsApp Support (+91 9921200935)

4. Information Required & What NEVER to Send

To locate your records, you only need to provide:

  • Your Full Name
  • Your 10-digit mobile number
  • Your email address (if authenticated via Google)
NEVER SEND PASSWORDS OR PRIVATE NUMBERS:
We will NEVER ask for your UAN password, OTP, Aadhaar number, or bank details. Do not include these in any deletion request.

5. What Happens Upon Deletion

  1. Verification: Our support desk verifies your ownership of the contact details within 48 business hours.
  2. Purging: Your personal profile, submitted inquiry notes, and communication threads are permanently deleted from our support inboxes and customer logs.
  3. Statutory Retentions: Please note that financial transaction records (such as payment IDs and tax invoice logs) may be retained for the minimum period mandated by Indian tax and accounting statutes before permanent disposal.
