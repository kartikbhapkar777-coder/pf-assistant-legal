Effective Date: October 26, 2026 • Published by AlgoAuraX for PF Pension Assistant

1. Overview & Policy Objective

AlgoAuraX maintains clear, fair, and prompt refund terms for the ₹299 PF Expert Assistance consultation. Every refund or cancellation request is handled fairly under the conditions specified below.

2. Refund Eligibility for the ₹299 Advisory Service

A full 100% refund of the ₹299 fee is granted in the following circumstances:

  • Unfulfilled Requests Prior to Consultation Review: Eligible if the user requests cancellation or the service request cannot be fulfilled prior to consultation review by our assistance specialist.
  • 48-Hour Response Commitment: Full refund if our assistance specialist fails to initiate guidance within 48 business hours of verified payment receipt.
  • Duplicate or Erroneous Transactions: Full refund if a user was debited multiple times for the same single inquiry due to network or payment gateway issues.

3. Non-Refundable Situations

  • Consultation Already Delivered: Once procedural analysis, document review, or joint declaration guidance has been drafted and delivered via WhatsApp or email, the advisory service is deemed completed and non-refundable.
  • Official Government Decisions: The ₹299 fee pays strictly for advisory time. Official claim rejection or pension delay by EPFO authorities due to employer non-contribution, service discrepancies, or statutory ineligibility does not qualify for a refund.

4. How to Submit a Refund Request

Users can submit a refund claim within 7 calendar days of transaction:

  1. Send an email to sachinvr141@gmail.com with the subject Refund Request - [Your Request ID], or message WhatsApp at +91 9921200935.
  2. Include your Full Name, 10-digit mobile number, Request ID (e.g. PF-REQ-XXXXX), and Razorpay transaction reference ID.

5. Settlement & Credit Timeline

Approved refunds are credited back to the original payment source (bank account, UPI, or card) through Razorpay within 5 to 7 business days, subject to bank processing cycles.
