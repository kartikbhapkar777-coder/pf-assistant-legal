Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: October 18, 2026 • Published by AlgoAuraX for PF Pension Assistant

Fee: ₹299 per Assistance Request Contact: sachinvr141@gmail.com

1. Overview & Fair Review Philosophy

At AlgoAuraX, we are committed to transparent, honest, and fair consumer relations. The PF Expert Assistance service is provided at a nominal fixed fee of ₹299 to assist users with individual PF and pension queries.

We do not apply a blanket "all fees are non-refundable" rule. Every refund or cancellation request is reviewed individually and decided fairly based on the criteria outlined below.

2. What the ₹299 Fee Pays For

The ₹299 service fee pays exclusively for personalized consultation time, procedural review, and navigational assistance delivered through our WhatsApp support team. It is a service fee for professional analysis and advisory.

3. Scenarios Eligible for a Full Refund

A 100% refund of the ₹299 fee will be granted under the following circumstances:

  • Duplicate or Multiple Charges: If you were accidentally charged twice or multiple times for the same single inquiry due to a network glitch or payment gateway error.
  • Payment Succeeded but Request Not Delivered: If your payment was deducted, but our system failed to generate a Request ID or our support desk failed to initiate contact within 48 hours of payment verification.
  • Cancellation Before Service Inception: If you submit a formal cancellation request via email or WhatsApp before our support specialist has reviewed your case documents or drafted an advisory response.
  • Service Ineligibility: If upon initial review, our team determines that your query falls outside the scope of EPF/EPS procedural guidance and we cannot provide assistance.

4. Scenarios Not Eligible for a Refund

Refunds cannot be granted in the following cases:

  • Advisory Already Delivered: Once our support specialist has reviewed your case and provided the personalized procedural advice, document guidance, or grievance draft via WhatsApp or Email, the service is deemed completed and non-refundable.
  • EPFO Authority Rejection or Outcome: The service fee covers procedural guidance only. If the EPFO department rejects your official claim due to employer issues, non-contributions, service gaps, or jurisdictional decisions beyond procedural control, this does not constitute grounds for a refund.
  • User Refusal to Cooperate: If a user refuses to provide basic non-sensitive information necessary to guide them or requests illegal interventions that violate EPFO rules.

5. How to Request a Refund

To submit a cancellation or refund request, please contact us within 7 days of your transaction:

  1. Send an email to sachinvr141@gmail.com with the subject line: Refund Request - [Your Request ID], or reach us on WhatsApp at +91 9921200935.
  2. Include your:
    • Full Name and registered 10-digit mobile number
    • Request ID (e.g., REQ-xxxxxx)
    • Payment Reference ID (provided by Razorpay or bank SMS)
    • Brief explanation of the reason for your refund request
  3. Our team will acknowledge your request within 24–48 business hours.

6. Refund Processing & Timelines

Once approved, refunds are credited back to the original payment source (bank account, UPI, or card) through our payment gateway (Razorpay). Depending on your bank's processing cycles, the amount typically reflects in your statement within 5 to 7 business days.
